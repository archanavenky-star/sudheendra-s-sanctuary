# Enlarged Reader Emblems and Series Index

## Changes
- Enlarge the fixed Articles, Insights, and Series emblems and reposition them so roughly two-thirds remain visible at the right edge on desktop and mobile.
- On every individual Series reading page, replace the generic left-side reading note with a sticky list of every part in that series.
- Mark the current part clearly and make every other part a direct link, while preserving the existing previous/next controls.
- Keep standard Article and Insight reading pages unchanged apart from the larger watermark.

## Validation
- Check an Article, Insight, and Series reader at desktop and mobile widths.
- Confirm the emblem stays fixed while scrolling, approximately two-thirds is visible, and no text is obscured.
- Confirm every series part appears in the left index, the active part is identified, and links open the correct part.
